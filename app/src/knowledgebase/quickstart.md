# Quickstart guide

Everything you need for your first ten minutes in the STEM Production Tracker. For the full reference, see the **Instruction manual**.

## 1. Sign in

1. Open the tracker's web address in Chrome, Edge or Safari.
2. Enter the email and temporary password your administrator gave you.
3. You'll be asked to **choose your own password** (at least 8 characters). After that you go straight to the dashboard.

Forgot your password? Ask an administrator to reset it. They'll give you a new temporary password.

## 2. Know your role

What you can change depends on your role. Everyone can see everything.

| Role | Can change |
|---|---|
| **Sales** | School and revenue on orders, the Pipeline, goals, simulations, weekly snapshots |
| **Operations** | Device PO, PCS received, serial numbers, ship and install dates, status, at-risk flag, room/teacher/contact details, builds, shortages, device shipments |
| **Accounting** | Billing status and bill month |
| **Admin** | Everything, plus the **Admin** tab (users, audit trail, backups) |

Notes on an order can be edited by every role.

## 3. Read the dashboard

The dashboard follows the same four sections as the old "2026 Story" sheet. Each colour means one thing:

- **Green**: done or money in (shipped, paid, collected).
- **Blue**: in progress and on track (billed, scheduled to ship).
- **Amber**: needs action (shipped but not billed, no bill month, shipped without devices).
- **Red**: at risk.
- **Purple**: forecast, not yet on a PO (Pipeline, Awaiting PO, Total Projected).
- **Grey**: for information, or nothing to do right now.

Amber and red cards turn grey when they're at zero, so a grey card means that item needs no action.

## 4. Find and update an order

1. Click **Orders**.
2. Pick the year, or type a school or S/O number in the search box and press Enter.
3. Click an order to open it on the right.
4. Change the fields your role allows (others are greyed out), then click **Save changes**.

Row colours: green = shipped, blue = shipped but devices outstanding, yellow = scheduled or in build, white = not yet scheduled, grey = next year, pink = no S/O yet. Non-lab orders (such as SnappyXO) are hidden unless you tick **Show all orders**.

Order number, date, school, revenue, labs, teacher stations and DOE PO come from **NetSuite** every 10 minutes. Change them in NetSuite, not here.

## 5. Add pipeline opportunities (Sales and Admin)

1. Click **Pipeline**.
2. Click **+ Add opportunity**, fill in the school, stage (Pipeline or Awaiting PO), estimated revenue and expected PO date, then **Save**.
3. To enter many at once, click **Download blank template**, fill it in Excel, then click **Upload template**, check the preview and click **Import**.

Pipeline and Awaiting PO totals appear on the dashboard and count toward **Total Projected**.

## 6. Get help

- **Knowledgebase → Instruction manual**: every screen explained.
- **Knowledgebase → Explainer video**: a three-minute tour.
- Something looks wrong? Tell an administrator. Every change is recorded in the audit trail, so they can see what happened and when.
